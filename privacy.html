RCW Finance Sync

Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy covers RCW Finance Sync (the "App"), a private bookkeeping tool operated by Rock Creek Wellness, LLC ("we"). It does not cover the clinic's website or patient care. For those, see the clinic's website privacy policy and Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information the App accesses

With QuickBooks accounting access, the App reads and writes accounting data in Rock Creek Wellness's own QuickBooks Online company: the chart of accounts, products and services, payment methods, sales transactions, and financial reports. When the App is connected, Intuit gives it the company's ID and access tokens, which it stores as described below. The App does not access QuickBooks Payments, payroll, bank logins, or any other company's data.

How it is used

Only to record and reconcile the clinic's own daily sales and to produce internal financial reports for Rock Creek Wellness.

Patient information

The App does not send patient names or other patient identifiers to QuickBooks. Entries it creates are daily totals by product, service, and payment type.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Data is kept on computers and secure services controlled by Rock Creek Wellness.
  • The App never sees or stores anyone's QuickBooks sign-in password. Its app keys and access tokens are encrypted in the computer's operating-system credential store and are never kept in plain text.
  • Data moves between the App and QuickBooks only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Access is limited to staff Rock Creek Wellness authorizes.

Security incidents

If we learn that information obtained through the App was accessed without authorization, we will act to contain it and will notify affected parties and Intuit as required by law and by Intuit's terms.

Sharing

We do not sell this information, share it for advertising, or give it to third parties for their own use. Service providers that help us operate the App (for example, secure hosting and software tools) may process it on our behalf under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information when required by law.

Retention

We keep accounting records as long as needed for the clinic's bookkeeping, tax, and legal obligations.

Your choices

The App's access can be ended at any time by disconnecting it in QuickBooks Online. See how to disconnect.

Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page.
