What it does

RCW Finance Sync is an internal tool built for and operated by Rock Creek Wellness, LLC, a medical clinic in Leawood, Kansas. It records the clinic's own daily sales summaries in the clinic's own QuickBooks Online company, and reads that company's accounting data to produce internal financial reports.

Who can use it

RCW Finance Sync is not offered to other businesses or to the public. Only authorized Rock Creek Wellness staff can connect it, and only to the clinic's own QuickBooks Online company, from computers the clinic controls.

What it does not do

  • It does not move money, take payments, run payroll, or send invoices.
  • It requests QuickBooks accounting access only.
  • It does not send patient names or other patient identifiers to QuickBooks. Entries are daily totals.

Connect or reconnect

An authorized clinic administrator starts the connection from the clinic's computer, signs in with Intuit, and approves access for the clinic's company. There is nothing to sign up for on this page.

Disconnect

In QuickBooks Online, open Apps, find RCW Finance Sync in your connected apps, and choose Disconnect. Once disconnected, RCW Finance Sync can no longer access the company. See what happens after you disconnect.
