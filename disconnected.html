RCW Finance Sync is disconnected

RCW Finance Sync no longer has access to your QuickBooks Online company. Any access tokens kept on the clinic's computer stop working immediately and cannot be used to read or change your books.

Transactions RCW Finance Sync recorded before you disconnected stay in QuickBooks. Disconnecting does not change or remove them.

To reconnect, an authorized clinic administrator starts the connection again from the clinic's computer.
